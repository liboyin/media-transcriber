# T12: Publish on filesystems without hard links

**Priority:** High. It must land before the Mac service publishes on the real share (T7 host checks, T11).

Evidence: T2's [SMB checks](T2.md#smb-hard-links) on 2026-11-18 showed that the Mac's SMB mount of the staging share refuses hard links and exclusive renames, and that a plain rename replaces files. T6's publication through `StagedFile` under `NEVER` therefore fails there with "Operation not supported".

Outcome: per DESIGN M21, publication falls back to a check followed by a rename where hard links are unsupported. The fallback is added to pathlib-extensions first, as a separate change in that repository with its own boundary, gates and review. This task then moves the lock to the new commit and uses the fallback in `media_transcriber/staging.py`.

Paths: `media_transcriber/staging.py`, `pyproject.toml`, `uv.lock`, tests, DESIGN section 16. Validation: tests that force the fallback on a filesystem with hard links, and a Mac host check that publishes one SRT on the real share (T2). Dependencies: the pathlib-extensions change, and the user's authorization to push it.

## Origin and boundary

Accepted for implementation on 2026-11-18, after the pathlib-extensions change landed as `f9adcc8` on its `main`. That change adds the keyword `rename_fallback` to `StagedFile`. It was reviewed in its own repository by a Codex reviewer at high effort, with no open findings.

- **Intent:** SRT publication works on the Mac's SMB mount of the staging share, as DESIGN M21 decides. Hard links stay in use wherever they work.
- **Dependencies:** DESIGN 5.6, M9, M20 and M21. pathlib-extensions `f9adcc8`. [T6](../closed/T6.md).
- **Implementation direction:** `publish_srt` passes `rename_fallback=True` to `StagedFile`. `uv.lock` moves pathlib-extensions from `b625087` to `f9adcc8`, and nothing else. The docstrings and DESIGN section 16 describe the fallback and its race.
- **Decisions within DESIGN's latitude (assumptions):** none beyond M21.
- **Compatibility surface:** none. Publication keeps its outcomes, except the replacement in the race that M21 accepts.
- **Non-goals:** a fallback for the library's `RENAME` policy, and any change to the checkpoint and directory checks of T6.
- **Validation:** tests that force unsupported hard links on a test-owned filesystem. They publish an absent SRT, keep an existing SRT, keep a competing SRT that appears before the final check, and run every checkpoint. Real hard links remain covered by T6's tests.
- **Mutation evidence:** without `rename_fallback`, publication fails when hard links are unsupported (the Mac's share cannot be published to).
- **Done:** the three gates, the review, a closed record, and DESIGN section 16. Publishing on the real Mac share is a host check in T2, run when the service exists (T7, T11).
